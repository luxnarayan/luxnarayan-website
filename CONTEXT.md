## Who he is

- Full name: Lakshmanan "Lux" Narayan. He goes by Lux.
- Founder and CEO of StreamAlive.
- Based in Basking Ridge, New Jersey.
- One-line bio on his current link page: "Founder | TED Speaker | Author | Interactive Presenter | Wannabee Comedian"
- The 3 values he organizes his life and work around: curiosity, humility, empathy.

## His story

- Born in Bombay. Early schooling there, then middle and high school in Dubai.
- IIT Madras, 1989–93.
- MBA from IIM Calcutta, 1993–95. He met Tina Lyngdoh there, now his wife and StreamAlive co-founder.
- His grandfather started the first public-speaking school in Madras (now Chennai) in the early 1970s. Lux counts him as his first teacher in presenting.
- 1998–2005: advertising and media in Dubai, at Lintas and then Initiative Media. He also taught at 2 Dubai universities during this time.
- 2005: moved to Chennai as part of the founding team at Vembu Technologies, a data backup software company. (Use this exact wording. Do not call him a co-founder of Vembu.)
- Founded Unmetric, a social media intelligence company. Moved to the US in 2013. Unmetric was acquired by Cision in late 2019.
- 2020: wrote his book, "Name, Place, Animal, Thing".
- While learning about publishing he took 6 paid courses across 56 livestreams. Every presenter either droned through slides, ignored the chat, or sent the audience to their phones, while the chat was on fire. That frustration became the idea for StreamAlive.
- 2021: founded StreamAlive with Tina Lyngdoh and Joe Varghese. Joe was his classmate at both IIT and IIM.

## StreamAlive

- What it is: audience-engagement software that turns the chat in a live session into real-time visuals such as polls, word clouds, maps and quizzes.
- Founded 2021. Headquartered in Basking Ridge, New Jersey.
- Co-founders: Lux Narayan (CEO), Tina Lyngdoh, Joe Varghese (CTO).
- What sets it apart: it runs on the chat people are already in. No QR code, no second app, no phone.
- Works with: Zoom, Microsoft Teams, Google Meet, YouTube Live, LinkedIn Live, Twitch, Webex.
- Also available through a PowerPoint add-in, a Chrome extension, and embeds in Gamma and Canva.
- Who uses it: trainers, educators, online instructors, webinar hosts.
- More than 10,000 presenters have tried StreamAlive.
- Zoom marketplace: #1 featured Zoom AI app, #1 featured Zoom education app, #1 featured Zoom employee engagement app. Zoom's App of the Month, February 2025.
- Largest audiences seen: 45,000 for a game show on YouTube, 5,000 for a company all-hands, 1,500 for education on YouTube. A typical trainer's session is about 30 people.
- Lux's own lines, usable verbatim:
  - "Unmute the audience."
  - "Stop one-way presentations."
  - "Everybody is a textrovert."
  - "Most webinars are borinars."
  - "The phone is a weapon of mass distraction."
- Link: https://www.streamalive.com

- A workshop that teaches people to mine their own lives for stories. It is built on the Name, Place, Animal, Thing framework from his book.
- Four steps: map out the mine, mine, polish, present.
- Who it is for: founders and executives.
- Corporate business matters most here. A private workshop for companies is available.
- The public workshop runs live on Zoom and is interactive, run on StreamAlive.
- All StoryMining visitors, corporate included, go to one place: https://storymining.life
